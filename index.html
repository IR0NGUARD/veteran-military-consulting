Frequently Asked Questions

Useful answers, upfront.

Can you consult remotely?

Yes. Script reviews, research calls, visual reviews, planning sessions, and many training engagements can be handled remotely.

Do you work with smaller projects?

Yes. Engagements range from one focused review to ongoing production support.

Is every military detail universal?

No. Practices vary by branch, unit, period, mission, and context. Recommendations identify those boundaries.

How are sensitive materials handled?

Discretion is expected, and confidentiality terms can be discussed before materials are shared.
